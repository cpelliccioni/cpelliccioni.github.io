Privacy Policy — +1 Floor

Last updated: 29/07/2026

+1Floor ("the app") is developed by Carlo Pelliccioni. This policy explains what data is handled when you use the app.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data ourselves. The app has no user accounts and no servers of our own. Game progress and settings are stored only on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service. AdMob may collect and process device information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, and usage data to serve and measure ads. This collection is governed by Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Purchasing the "Remove ads" option permanently disables all advertising in the app.

Game Center

Leaderboards use Apple's Game Center. If you are signed in, your Game Center nickname and scores are processed by Apple to display rankings, under Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/. We do not receive or store this data.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive any payment or billing information.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address.
